# Sauti Yako Logo Concept

## Concept

The mark combines a **microphone** with a **protective shield/speech silhouette** to represent voice, listening, reporting, and institutional safety. Two interlocking abstract human forms express inclusion, solidarity, and equality without relying on literal gender glyphs. The palm, sun, and ocean-wave gestures provide subtle Zanzibar and SUZA-inspired context while keeping the identity distinct from the official SUZA crest.

## Suggested palette

| Color | Suggested use | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Deep navy | Primary mark and wordmark | `#0B3B70` |
| Turquoise | Ocean waves and inclusion accent | `#16B7C5` |
| Palm green | Zanzibar/nature accent | `#56A83A` |
| Academic gold | Sun and institutional highlight | `#F2B515` |

## Usage guidance

Use the full-color lockup on a white or very light background for formal reports, campus communications, social profiles, and awareness materials. For small applications, embroidery, stamps, or single-color printing, use a simplified navy one-color version of the symbol and wordmark. Keep clear space around the mark and avoid stretching, adding effects, or placing it over busy photography.

## Important identity note

This is a **concept logo** inspired by the visual language of Zanzibar and SUZA; it is not the official SUZA emblem and should be reviewed for institutional approval, trademark clearance, and final vector redrawing before formal adoption or registration.
